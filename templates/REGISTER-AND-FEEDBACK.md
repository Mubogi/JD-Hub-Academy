# Attendance Register & Feedback Form

Two operational sheets. Print both. Paper works better than a spreadsheet in the
room — you cannot chase a laptop around a lab, and a form handed in at the door
gets a far higher response rate than a link sent afterwards.

---

State this at enrolment so it is a rule, not a punishment invented on the day.

## 2. End-of-course feedback form

Hand out at the start of session 12, collect before certificates. Ten questions
maximum — longer forms get abandoned.

```
JD HUB ACADEMY — COURSE FEEDBACK

Course: ______________________________  Date: ____________

Circle one per line.      1 = strongly disagree   5 = strongly agree

1. The course met the description I enrolled for.        1  2  3  4  5
2. The facilitator explained things clearly.             1  2  3  4  5
3. The exercises used my real work, not samples.         1  2  3  4  5
4. The pace was right for me.                            1  2  3  4  5
5. The course book was useful during sessions.           1  2  3  4  5
6. I can use what I learned in my job this week.         1  2  3  4  5
7. I would recommend this course to a colleague.         1  2  3  4  5

8. The most useful thing I learned was:
   _______________________________________________________________________

9. The one thing that should change is:
   _______________________________________________________________________

10. Would you take another JD Hub course?   YES / NO
    If yes, which topic? ______________________________________________

Name (optional): ____________________  Phone: ____________________

May we quote your feedback publicly?   YES / NO
```

**Why question 10 matters more than the rest.** It is your pipeline. Every "yes"
is a warm lead for the next tier. Follow up within a week, while the course is
still fresh.

**The two questions that actually improve the course** are 8 and 9. Read every
answer to 9 before the next cohort. The rest is reassurance; those two are
information.
