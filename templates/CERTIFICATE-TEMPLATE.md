# Certificate Templates

Two certificates. The completion certificate is issued on **passing the rubric**,
not on attendance — state that at enrolment and again in session one. The
distinction is what makes the certificate worth something.

For a print-ready A4 landscape design, use `scripts/build_certificate.py`.
This file is the text specification.

---

## 1. Certificate of Completion

```
                          JD HUB ACADEMY
                     Training for the real economy

                        CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION

  This is to certify that

                          [LEARNER FULL NAME]

  has successfully completed

                     [COURSE TITLE] ([COURSE CODE])

  a [duration] programme comprising [X] contact hours, and has been assessed
  against the published rubric for this course.

  Awarded this [day] day of [month], [year]



  ____________________________          ____________________________
  Mubogi Gastavas Jordan                [Assessor name]
  President, JD Hub Tech                Assessor

  Certificate number: JDHA-[CODE]-[YEAR]-[NNN]
  Verify at: [verification email or URL]
```

## 2. Certificate of Attendance

For learners who attended but did not sit or pass the assessment. It must say
**attendance**, not completion. Never blur the two — the value of the completion
certificate depends on it.

```
                          JD HUB ACADEMY

                        CERTIFICATE OF ATTENDANCE

  This certifies that

                          [LEARNER FULL NAME]

  attended the [X]-session programme

                     [COURSE TITLE] ([COURSE CODE])

  held at [venue] from [start date] to [end date].

  Awarded this [day] day of [month], [year]



  ____________________________
  Mubogi Gastavas Jordan
  President, JD Hub Tech

  Certificate number: JDHA-[CODE]-[YEAR]-[NNN]
```

---

## Numbering

`JDHA-[COURSE CODE]-[YEAR]-[NNN]`

Example: `JDHA-AI101-2026-014` — fourteenth AI-101 completion certificate issued
in 2026.

Keep a single register of every certificate issued: number, learner name, course,
date, assessor, and rubric result. A register is what lets you verify a
certificate when an employer calls — and they do call.

## Printing

- A4 landscape, 200gsm minimum. Thin paper undermines the certificate.
- Print the learner's name, never handwrite it.
- Signature in blue ink, by hand, after printing. It takes ten seconds and it is
  the difference between a document and a souvenir.
- Issue within seven days of the capstone. Late certificates feel like an
  afterthought because they are one.

## What the rubric pass means

The rubric is printed in the course book, Module 13, Lesson 13.2. A learner
passes by presenting a working workflow that:

1. Saves measurable time on a real task.
2. Uses at least three techniques from the course.
3. Can be demonstrated in five minutes.
4. Would still be in use in ninety days.

A learner who attends all twelve sessions but cannot demonstrate a working
workflow does not receive the completion certificate. Say this clearly and early.
